## Business analysis & workflow focus

My professional practice centres on:

- **Workflow analysis:** examine current processes, handoffs, bottlenecks, and opportunities to reduce manual effort.
- **Requirements and business rules:** turn stakeholder needs into clear system behaviour and acceptance criteria.
- **Data integrity:** reconcile information, validate KPI logic, and investigate inconsistencies before reporting.
- **Automation:** identify repeatable steps and define the rules, controls, and human review a solution needs.
- **Stakeholder communication:** explain decisions, findings, and tradeoffs in language people can act on.

The school projects below demonstrate the technical foundation supporting this work. Each project identifies its own implemented scope and evidence.

## Featured work

### 01 · Responsible AI: Fairness & Explainability

**Question:** Can strong overall model accuracy conceal different outcomes across groups?

Logistic Regression, Fairlearn, SHAP, and LIME are used to examine income classification. The saved run reports 85.37% accuracy alongside differences in group selection and error rates.

**Portfolio value:** Demonstrates performance analysis, explainability, and careful interpretation of AI risks.

[Explore the project →](https://github.com/Ridwat/Ethical-AI-Analysis)

### 02 · Historical Stock Market Analysis

**Question:** How do historical price and volume distributions differ across decades?

An exploratory workflow combines price records with stock metadata and creates time-based summaries and visualizations.

**Portfolio value:** Demonstrates data preparation, joins, analytical reporting, and explicit limitations.

[Explore the project →](https://github.com/Ridwat/Project-1-Stock-Market-Data-Analysis)

### 03 · Wine Clustering & Segmentation

**Question:** Can high-dimensional measurements reveal useful groups without labels?

K-Means and agglomerative clustering are compared on standardized Wine data. The saved run selects three clusters; two PCA components capture 55.4% of variance.

**Portfolio value:** Demonstrates segmentation, model comparison, and visual interpretation.

[Explore the project →](https://github.com/Ridwat/unsupervised-LEARNING-assignment)

### 04 · Fashion-MNIST Neural Network

**Question:** Which clothing categories can a dense neural network distinguish reliably?

A TensorFlow/Keras model achieves 88.91% accuracy in its saved test run, with class-level analysis showing where it struggles.

**Portfolio value:** Demonstrates neural-network training, validation, and error analysis.

[Explore the project →](https://github.com/Ridwat/Neural-Network)

### 05 · Titanic Exploratory Analysis

**Question:** How do survival patterns vary across passenger groups?

Data preparation, family-size features, grouped summaries, and visualizations reveal descriptive differences in the passenger data.

**Portfolio value:** Demonstrates a clear progression from raw data to interpretable findings.

[Explore the project →](https://github.com/Ridwat/Lesson_5_Titanic_EDA)

### 06 · Image Classification Baseline

**Question:** How well can a classical model classify flattened image pixels?

Random Forest tuning and class-level evaluation produce 66.13% test accuracy in the saved run on a small five-class dataset.

**Portfolio value:** Demonstrates baseline construction, hyperparameter tuning, and honest performance assessment.

[Explore the project →](https://github.com/Ridwat/image-classification-random-forest)
